# 📊 Sales & Profit Performance Dashboard

An interactive Tableau dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, profitability, regional performance, product performance, customer segments, shipping modes, and sales trends over time.

The project demonstrates how transactional sales data can be transformed into an interactive business intelligence dashboard for decision-making.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The objective of this project was to build an interactive **Sales & Profit Performance Dashboard** that provides a high-level overview of business performance while allowing users to drill into different business dimensions.

The dashboard helps answer key business questions such as:

- What are the total sales and profit?
- How many orders and units were sold?
- Which region generates the highest sales?
- Which category contributes most to sales and profit?
- Which products generate high sales and high profit?
- Which customer segment contributes the most revenue?
- Which shipping mode is most frequently used?
- How does sales performance change over time?

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The primary objective is to provide management with a single interactive dashboard for monitoring sales and profitability and identifying areas that require further investigation.

The dashboard focuses on:

- Sales performance
- Profitability

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

### 1. Technology is the strongest category

Technology generates the highest sales and profit among the three categories.

**Business implication:**

Management should continue monitoring demand, inventory availability, pricing, discounts, and product-level profitability within Technology.

---

### 2. West is the strongest region

West generates the highest sales among the four regions.

**Business implication:**

The business should maintain service quality and inventory availability in this region while investigating opportunities for growth in lower-performing regions.

---

### 3. Consumer is the largest segment

Consumer contributes approximately **51% of total sales**.

**Business implication:**

The Consumer segment represents a major revenue base and should be monitored closely while identifying opportunities to improve Corporate and Home Office performance.

---

### 4. Standard Class is the dominant shipping mode

Standard Class accounts for the largest portion of sales and order volume.

**Business implication:**

Management should ensure sufficient operational capacity and maintain service quality for the dominant shipping mode.

---

### 5. Product-level profitability requires investigation

The Sales vs Profit scatter plot highlights differences between revenue generation and profitability.

A product with high sales does not necessarily mean it has an equally strong profit performance.

**Potential areas for investigation:**

- Pricing
- Discounts
- Product costs
- Product mix
- Profit margins
